# OT-POL-003 Low Impact Cyber Security Plan

## 2.1
This plan implements CIP-003-8 Attachment 1. The organization maintains cyber
security awareness reinforcement at least once every 15 calendar months for its
low impact BES Cyber Systems, per CIP-003-8 Requirement R2.
