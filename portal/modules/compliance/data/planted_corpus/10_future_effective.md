# OT-POL-013 Supply Chain Risk Management Policy

## 1.1
The organization operates one or more processes, used in planning for the
procurement of BES Cyber Systems and their associated EACMS and PACS, to
identify and assess cyber security risk to the Bulk Electric System from vendor
products or services, including procuring and installing vendor equipment and
software and transitions between vendors.
