# OT-POL-007 Security Patch Management Policy

## 4.2
The organization shall evaluate security patches for applicability at least once
every 35 calendar days, assessing every patch released by the tracked patch
sources since the last evaluation. The patch evaluation is assigned to the
Systems Security team and recorded in the patch register.
