## What the current code does

- **Auto-council** in `config/portal.yaml` has three isolated, one-shot reviewers (Evidence Auditor, Challenger, Operator/User Advocate). It applies a common structured review contract, collects votes, then calls a fourth Qwen3.8 synthesizer. `minimum_participation: 0.66` and `quorum: 0.66` each round up to two of three; abstentions, failures, and invalid outputs do not vote. The synthesizer can explain the coded outcome and preserve dissent/missing evidence; it cannot change the outcome. This is a useful separation of judgement from prose.
- **Compliance council** has three sequential sealed calls from `portal/modules/compliance/core/council.py`, all receiving the same `_SEAT_SYSTEM` and pre-analyzed JSON packet. It checks citations against an allowlist, drops malformed or invalid votes, uses the full three-seat denominator, and escalates unless one determination gets two votes. An unmet decision may trigger a separate exception-override pass using the roster. It has no narrative synthesizer.
- The compliance roles are not currently distinct at prompt level. Add short, functional review lenses (evidence/applicability, neutral obligation mapping, strongest counterexample/exception) while keeping one shared authority, identical allowed verdicts, and the current JSON schema. Avoid character/personality personas and forced “always reject” instructions. First compare any lens change separately from a model change.

## What 2025–2026 research supports

**Short answer:** research supports isolated structured review as a plausible error-detection aid, and supports testing different capabilities/lineages when common-mode errors matter. It does not support the stronger claim that a multi-model council, role prompt, debate round, larger panel, confidence-weighted vote, or LLM synthesizer will generally be more accurate. Findings differ by task and budget.

### Roles, heterogeneity, and panel size

- The 2025 A-HMAD study reports 4–6 percentage-point gains over standard debate on six reasoning/factual tasks; identical-agent ablations lost up to 3.5 points on reasoning, and learned consensus added 5 points when initial votes split. It uses a particular Llama-2-70B framework with specialized agents, dynamic routing, and learned aggregation; those results do not transfer directly to these local models or compliance packets. ([Zhou & Chen, 24 Nov 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44443-025-00353-3))
- The 2026 small-model study tests 23 models from 11 families across five tasks and 5,500+ runs. Debate beat a single response by 3–7 points where tasks had headroom, but at matched generation budgets it tied or lost to self-consistency while costing 1.6× wall time and 3.4× tokens. Persona prompting reduced accuracy; mixed-model teams lost to majority votes over their own roster. This is the strongest caution against assuming that three labelled roles or family diversity alone produce a gain. It is a Sep 2026 preprint submitted to ICLR 2027 and is focused on small open models. ([Ferreira et al., 26 Sep 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35875))
- A 2025 systematic critique compared five debate methods, nine benchmarks, and four base models; it found debate often failed to beat CoT/self-consistency at greater inference cost, while its heterogeneity experiments supported heterogeneous members in its tested setups. Treat that and the 2026 result as mixed evidence, not as a universal vote for or against diversity. ([Zhang et al., v3 dated 21 Jun 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08788))
- A 2026 controlled study of homogeneous teams of 7–8B models on GSM-Hard and MMLU-Hard observed majority-answer adoption up to 85.5%, a 32.3-point oracle gap, and 2.1–3.4× token use for unguided debate versus isolated self-correction. This supports the councils' initial isolation and argues against exposing sealed reviewers to one another's rationales before they vote. ([Bertalanič & Fortuna, 29 Apr 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00914))
- These results do not establish an optimal panel size. Keep three voters: it gives a simple two-of-three decision and already consumes substantial resident memory. Do not add a fourth or fifth voter without a task-specific, budget-matched gain. The local fan-out record measures the present auto-council reviewers at 50.3 GiB for one slot and 60.2 GiB at two slots (`docs/TASK_FANOUT_CONCURRENCY_V1.md`, 2026-09-25); this is a memory/capacity warning, not a quality measurement.

### Self-consistency, reasoning, and role prompts

- On 900 human-scored high-school math conversations, a 2026 study found temperature sampling improved scoring accuracy, increasing self-consistency from one to seven samples did not give significant gains, and higher reasoning effort showed a positive trend. This is a narrow scoring task and does not settle reasoning settings for document-grounded council review. ([Frohn, 3 Apr 2026; presented at NCME Apr 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26954))
- EMNLP Findings 2025 finds agent-dialogue diversity tends to decay over long simulations and that reducing contextual prompt information can increase diversity. That studies simulated dialogue diversity, not council correctness; do not omit evidence from these reviewers to manufacture disagreement. ([Chu et al., Nov 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1397/))
- Use compact, task-functional lenses and give every reviewer enough internal reasoning to check evidence, applicability, and counterexamples. Do not require exposed chain-of-thought; request concise evidence references and a short rationale in the existing schema. Keep the Qwen3.8 synthesizer non-thinking/non-voting unless a same-budget task test proves an advantage. For voter thinking settings, compare per-model settings under identical prompts, caps, route, and total budget; do not turn thinking on uniformly by assumption.

### Voting, calibration, and synthesis

- A 2026 judge study tested four benchmarks, six models, and 36 judge/examinee pairs. Judge task accuracy tracked model capability (Pearson r ≥ 0.90 for most models), but more capable examinees received more lenient scores (r ≥ 0.83). Its calibrated weighted majority came within 0.5 percentage points of an oracle in simulated distribution shifts. The calibration result is a simulation, not evidence to replace this code's equal vote with guessed weights. ([Zhang et al., 10 Sep 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12002))
- A 2025 ICLR study identifies 12 judge biases and finds that stronger judges still retain material bias on some tasks. Keep candidate/response order and identity controlled in any evaluation, and score each judge's false-positive/false-negative and abstention behavior on frozen labels before considering weighting. ([Ye et al., ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/fdca08d371e4b6c031397909e20043bd-Abstract-Conference.html))
- Retain the existing deterministic 2/3 quorum, full-roster denominator, abstain/error handling, and escalation on disagreement. Do not weight by a model's self-reported confidence; auto-council currently parses confidence but does not need to use it as vote weight. On high-impact cases, preserve material dissent and route unresolved risk to a human or an explicit escalation path even if a bare 2/3 majority exists.
- Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) is a layered generation/refinement method: later agents see earlier outputs. Its 2025 ICLR paper reports 65.1% AlpacaEval 2.0 for its open-source MoA versus 57.5% for GPT-4 Omni. This supports testing a constrained synthesis stage for prose quality, not letting a language model replace a deterministic verdict. Keep auto-council's Qwen3.8 synthesizer strictly post-vote and non-voting; keep compliance's no-synthesizer design. ([Wang et al., ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/5434be94e82c54327bb9dcaf7fca52b6-Abstract-Conference.html))

## Cheapest local checks before any seat change

The 2026-10-18 census says there is no current WFE instrument for auto-council or these three compliance-council seats. `tests/benchmarks/bench_judgment_probe_v6.py` exists and is the cheapest compliance screen, but before using it for a promotion decision, reconcile it against the actual `assess_part` route and council contract. Preserve each current incumbent as its own arm; freeze candidate prompts and holdout; do not combine a model swap and prompt rewrite in one comparison.

1. **Auto-council candidates and Qwen3.8 synthesis:** use a small, human-labeled task set with stable expected outcome/task IDs spanning supported, contradicted, stale/ambiguous, missing-evidence, operational-risk, and split-vote cases. Run the actual public `auto-council` path with current members vs one changed member at a time, same content, context, output cap, sampling and resolved model identities. Score evidence precision/recall, false accepts/rejects, abstentions, JSON validity, dissent preservation, and final explanation fidelity to the machine decision. Capture memory at each seat's real context and confirm every resident is ≤35 GB; also record the co-resident set. There is no council WFE fixture or receipt to reuse today.
2. **Compliance-council Qwen, Granite, and GLM candidate:** start with `bench_judgment_probe_v6.py` as a cheap screen, then perform the incumbent/candidate comparison through the real `assessment.assess_part` → production council route on a separate frozen holdout. Match packet, references/allowlist, seat contract, JSON enforcement, thinking settings, 8192-token budget, parser/drop rules, and temperature; retain raw requests, reasoning channel, final outputs, and route identity. Score determination accuracy, abstain recall, schema, citation precision, and quorum/escalation. Keep the 2-of-3 denominator fixed. Diagnose the P3B failure before removing DeepSeek-R1; do not infer a model-global limitation from the prior arm.
3. **Compliance role lenses:** only after the model comparison, A/B the narrow evidence, semantic, and counterexample lenses against the identical shared-prompt baseline on the same frozen cases. Measure each seat's unique correct findings and correlated misses, not just vote agreement. Keep an “allow no counterexample” instruction so the counterexample role does not manufacture objections.
4. **Memory and route gate:** before any promotion, verify the actual pipeline-resolved backend/model, target context, runtime residency including KV, and raw response schema on the same box. Weight-file sizes and vendor scorecards do not establish the ≤35 GB seat rule or production-path correctness.
